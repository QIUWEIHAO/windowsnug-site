Accessibility permission

macOS only lets an app move another app's windows if you grant Accessibility access. On first launch Window Snug asks for it and opens the right settings pane for you: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility. Turn Window Snug on and you're set.

Privacy

Window Snug collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no network requests. Your shortcut preferences stay on your Mac. Read the privacy policy.
